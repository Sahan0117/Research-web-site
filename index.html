Safe & Accurate Sri Lankan Medicinal Plant Authentication

Abstract: The misidentification of Sri Lankan medicinal plants poses a severe health risk, as highly utilized Ayurvedic herbs are frequently confused with visually similar toxic substitutes. This project presents a cohesive, AI-driven digital ecosystem designed to authenticate medicinal plants, securely manage pharmacological data, and facilitate institutional research. The solution integrates an Authentication Engine (EfficientNetB0 with Out-of-Distribution rejection), a Digital Herbarium (MongoDB), and a Batch Analytics module. It prioritizes user safety through explicit 'Visha Haranaya' warnings, achieving an 86.9% accuracy and paving the way for safe digitization of indigenous knowledge.

Domain Information

Research Problem

Accidental poisoning due to the misidentification of medicinal plants with toxic look-alikes, worsened by the lack of domain-specific constraints in generic botanical apps.

Objectives

1. High-accuracy classification with toxic weed rejection.
2. Secure storage and retrieval of pharmacological profiles.
3. Asynchronous batch processing for bulk datasets.

Technologies Used

EfficientNetB0, Grad-CAM, FastAPI, MongoDB (NoSQL), Celery Task Queues, React.js
